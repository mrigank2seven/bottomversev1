06 / OUR STORY

BUILT FOR
THE UNBOXED.

Bottomverse is an independent everyday-wear label from Bareilly, Uttar Pradesh. Made for people who want their clothes to feel like them.

WHY WE STARTED

BASIC WAS NEVER THE POINT.

We started Bottomverse because the everyday tee deserved more thought. More weight. Better proportions. A fit that gives you room to move without disappearing inside the clothes.

Every piece starts with the same question: would we actually reach for this tomorrow? If the answer is no, it does not make the cut.

01 / THE PRODUCT

Take up space

Heavyweight cotton, intentional volume, and silhouettes that hold their shape.

02 / THE PROCESS

Make it count

Small drops, considered details, and no filler for the sake of filling a rack.

03 / THE ATTITUDE

Stay unboxed

Wear it your way. The best uniform is the one that leaves room for you.
